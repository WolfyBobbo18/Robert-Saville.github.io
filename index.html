Capping Project – Puzzle UI & Interface Design

As part of my capstone game project, I designed and refined user interface systems that supported player exploration and puzzle solving throughout the game.

My work focused on creating clear, intuitive interfaces that communicated gameplay information without distracting from the player experience. Using Figma, I iterated on multiple UI concepts, refining card layouts, exploration menus, and interaction systems based on feedback and testing.

The final implementation included a puzzle solving interface integrated directly into gameplay, along with supporting menus and navigation elements that helped create a cohesive visual experience across the project.

Campus Connect – UI Prototype

Campus Connect is a mobile application prototype designed to help first-year college students transition more smoothly to campus life by providing essential information and services in a single, accesible platform.

The project was created as part of a UX design course and focused on improving the student experience during their first year. Through user centered design principles, I explored ways to reduce confusion and improve access to important campus resources.

Key features include access to class schedules, a digital campus map, campus points of interest with students ratings and reviews, and shuttle system information and transportation access. The goal was to replace fragmented information sources with a more intuitive and convenient mobile experience that helps students navigate campus, discover services, and feel more connected to their community.

Tools: Figma
