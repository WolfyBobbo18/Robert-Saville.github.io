Project Overview

Campus Connect was a mobile application concept designed to improve the first-year student experience by helping freshmen navigate campus life, discover resources, and connect with other students.

The project focused on improving access to existing campus services that were often underutilized because students were unaware they existed or found them difficult to locate.

Using a mobile-first approach, the application brought together campus navigation, transportation information, scheduling tools, and student engagement features into a single platform.

Design Challenge

Many campus resources already existed but were not being fully utilized by students.

Some resources were difficult to discover, while others relied on outdated systems. For example, the university's campus map was still presented as a static image, making navigation difficult for students unfamiliar with campus.

The challenge was to create a centralized mobile experience that would make important resources easier to discover and use.

Proposed Solution

Campus Connect was designed as a single hub for campus information and student engagement.

Key features included:

  • Interactive Campus Map
  • Bus Transit Information
  • Schedule Management
  • Student Connection Features
  • Campus Resource Discovery
  • Points of Interest
  • Ratings & Reviews

By bringing these systems together, students could spend less time searching for information and more time engaging with campus life.

Interface Design

The application focused on simplicity and accessibility. Screens were designed to provide quick access to information while maintaining a clean and approachable visual style.

The design prioritized:

  • Ease of Navigation
  • Readability
  • Resource Discovery
  • Mobile Accessibility

Design Process

Using Figma, I developed prototype screens that demonstrated how students could navigate between major campus resources.

The goal was not to build a fully functional application but to explore how improved information architecture and mobile design could increase awareness and usage of existing campus services.

Tools Used

  • Figma

Outcome

Campus Connect demonstrated how a centralized mobile platform could improve the student experience by making campus resources more visible, accessible, and easier to use.

The project strengthened my skills in UX design, mobile interface design, information architecture, user-centered design, and interactive prototyping.
