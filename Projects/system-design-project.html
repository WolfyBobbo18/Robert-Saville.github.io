Project Overview

This project was completed as part of a team-based Systems Design course. Our client was a local blacksmith who primarily sold products through Facebook Marketplace and wanted a dedicated website to better showcase his products and services.

The goal was to design a professional website that would allow customers to browse products, learn about available services, and improve the overall online presence of the business.

Team Collaboration

I worked closely with teammates and the client throughout the project, helping translate business goals into a functional website design. While other team members created portions of the DFDs and Context Diagrams, I contributed to planning, requirements development, interface design, and project documentation.

Design Challenge

The client relied heavily on Facebook Marketplace to manage sales and customer communication. This created limitations in branding, organization, and customer accessibility.

Our challenge was to design a website that would provide a more professional online presence while remaining easy for customers to navigate and for the client to manage.

Research & Requirements Gathering

Early stages of the project focused on understanding the client's business needs and identifying key website requirements.

Through meetings and discussions with the client, our team gathered information about products, services, customer expectations, and desired website functionality.

Deliverables included:

  • Functional Requirements
  • Non-Functional Requirements
  • Use Cases
  • System Documentation

Information Architecture

One of my primary responsibilities was helping organize the website's structure and navigation.

I created site maps and user flow diagrams to visualize how visitors would move through the website and access important information.

These planning documents helped ensure the final design remained intuitive and aligned with both user needs and client goals.

Wireframing & UI Design

Using Figma, I developed wireframes and interface mockups that served as the foundation for the final website prototype.

This design focused on presenting customization options clearly, highlighting services, and creating a clean navigation experience for visitors.

Multiple iterations were created throughout development as feedback was gathered from teammates and the client.

Prototype Development

The final website prototype was developed using Lovable and combined the planning, requirements analysis, and UI designs produced throughout the project.

The completed prototype provided product listings, service information, and a professional digital storefront experience for the client.

Outcome

The final prototype successfully addressed the client's needs by providing a more professional and organized online presence than relying solely on Facebook Marketplace.

This project strengthened my skills in UI/UX design, systems analysis, requirements gathering, information architecture, and client-focused design processes.
