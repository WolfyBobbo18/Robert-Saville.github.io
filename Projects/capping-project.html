Project Overview

The Capping Project was a team-based game development capstone focused on player exploration, puzzle solving, and environmental storytelling. Players traveled between islands collecting crystals and ultimately reuniting them under the Mother Crystal's guidance.

Throughout development, my role evolved from early concept art contributions into a primary focus on UI design and quality assurance testing.

My Role

UI/UX Design

  • Exploration Interaction UI
  • Dialogue Box Design
  • Interface Iteration & Testing

Quality Assurance

  • Gameplay Testing
  • Bug Identification
  • Usability Feedback

Early Art Contributions

At the beginning of development, I contributed concept artwork for several game assets and characters before transitioning into UI design, where the majority of my final contributions were implemented.

Design Challenge

The game's interface needed to provide players with important information without disrupting exploration or immersion. Because puzzles and environmental interaction were central to gameplay, the UI had to remain intuitive, readable, and visually consistent with the game's fantasy setting.

Puzzle Interface Design

My primary contribution was the design of the rune-based puzzle interface.

The puzzle system required players to manipulate rune cards and interact with magical mechanics. To achieve this, I developed multiple interface concepts and iterated through three separate versions of the rune card designs before arriving at the final implementation.

Additional UI elements I designed included:

  • Mana Container
  • Reset Button
  • Rune Card Variations
  • Puzzle Interaction Layout

Each iteration focused on improving readability, player understanding, and visual clarity during puzzle solving.

Exploration Interface

To support world interaction, I designed the exploration prompt system used throughout gameplay.

This interface functioned similarly to a traditional "Press E to Interact" prompt, providing players with clear feedback when they approached interactive objects and locations.

The goal was to communicate interaction opportunities without cluttering the screen or distracting from exploration.

Dialogue System

I designed custom dialogue boxes that visually reflected each island's unique identity.

Each island received its own colored gradient dialogue box treatment, helping reinforce environmental themes and giving conversations a stronger connection to the surrounding world.

This allowed the user interface to contribute to the game's storytelling rather than simply presenting text.

Design Process

My UI workflow centered around iterative design and feedback.

Using Figma, I created multiple interface concepts and refined them through testing and team feedback. Each revision focused on improving usability while maintaining visual consistency across the game's systems.

Key considerations included:

  • Readability
  • Visual Consistency
  • Player Feedback
  • Puzzle Clarity
  • Immersion

Tools Used

  • Figma
  • Unity

Quality Assurance

Following completion of my UI work, I joined the Quality Assurance team to assist with testing and polish.

Responsibilities included:

  • Identifying Bugs
  • Testing Gameplay Systems
  • Providing Usability Feedback
  • Verifying Interface Functionality

This experience provided additional insight into how players interacted with the systems I helped design.

Outcome

The final UI implementation supported puzzle solving, exploration, and narrative interactions while maintaining a cohesive visual style throughout the game.

The project strengthened my skills in UI/UX design, interface iteration, usability testing, and collaborative game development.
